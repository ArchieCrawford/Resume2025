# Asset credits

Landmark buildings, the house, and the desk are third-party models. Company names on the signs and the road are plain text from `resume.json`. No employer logos are used.

The drivable kart, wheels, road, curbs, ground lettering, arrows, cones, crates, beach balls, pins, ramp, and bollards are original geometry made for this circuit. They are not taken from Bruno Simon's portfolio or from any other driving demo.

## Decoder

Neighborhood models are meshopt-compressed. The Meshopt decoder shipped in `world/vendor/meshopt_decoder.module.js` is from the three.js r160 examples and is MIT licensed (meshoptimizer, Arseny Kapoulkine).
